Season Gantt

Every storm in a season as a class-colored bar across the calendar, sorted by genesis.

Bars span each storm's tropical or subtropical life at 6-hour resolution. Gaps are extratropical or remnant phases. Storms are attributed to their genesis season.

Data: NHC HURDAT2 · NOAA NCEI IBTrACS v04r01 · live ATCF b-decks. Built for weather nerds by a weather nerd.
