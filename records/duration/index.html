Duration & Motion Records

Storm-level extremes in lifetime, time at strength, track distance, forward speed, and latitude.

Duration, distance, and latitude records depend on complete track coverage. Seasons before the satellite era can miss storm segments, so early-era durations and distances are lower bounds.

Data: NHC HURDAT2 · NOAA NCEI IBTrACS v04r01 · live ATCF b-decks. Built for weather nerds by a weather nerd.
