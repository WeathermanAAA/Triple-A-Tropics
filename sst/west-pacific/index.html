About

The Western Pacific warm pool — typically the warmest large body of open water on Earth — keeps the region primed for tropical development year-round. SST anomalies here often correlate with El Niño / La Niña state and modulate where typhoons form and how intense they become.

Source: NOAA OISST v2.1 daily analysis. Baseline for anomalies is the 1991–2020 mean; records are compared against the full OISST era (1982–present).
