About

Because the Niño 3.4 region sits inside this view, East Pacific anomaly maps make El Niño / La Niña state particularly visible: El Niño years paint the equatorial strip warm, La Niña cool. The basin's hurricane activity tracks these patterns tightly.

Source: NOAA OISST v2.1 daily analysis. Baseline for anomalies is the 1991–2020 mean; records are compared against the full OISST era (1982–present).
