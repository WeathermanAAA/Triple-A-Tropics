About

Atlantic SST directly fuels tropical cyclone intensification. Waters above ~26.5 °C can support tropical development; the Atlantic main development region (the belt from 10–20 °N across the central Atlantic) warms through summer and peaks in September, matching the climatological peak of hurricane activity.

Source: NOAA OISST v2.1 daily analysis. Baseline for anomalies is the 1991–2020 mean; records are compared against the full OISST era (1982–present).
