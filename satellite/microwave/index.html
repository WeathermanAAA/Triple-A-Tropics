Passive Microwave (Observed)

Observed passive-microwave overpasses of active and recent tropical cyclones in four canonical NRL products: the 91 GHz color composite and 91H brightness temperature (the high-frequency scattering channels, which cut through cirrus to show convective structure and the eyewall), and the 37 GHz color composite and 37H brightness temperature (which highlight low-level rain bands and a forming eye). Live overpasses come from NASA GPM/PPS near-real-time (~1–3 h after the pass); older storms from the NOAA/CIRA TC-PRIMED archive. For the scatterometer ocean-surface winds, see the Scatterometer tab.
