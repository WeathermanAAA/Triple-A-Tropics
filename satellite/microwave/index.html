Passive Microwave (Observed)

Observed passive-microwave overpasses of active and recent tropical cyclones — 89 GHz PCT (polarization-corrected temperature, which cuts through cirrus to show the storm's convective structure and eyewall) and 37 GHz color (which highlights the low-level rain bands and a forming eye). Live overpasses come from NASA GPM/PPS near-real-time (~1–3 h after the pass); older storms from the NOAA/CIRA TC-PRIMED archive. For the scatterometer ocean-surface winds, see the Scatterometer tab.
