Subseasonal Drivers

The planetary-scale background the tropics ride on: the MJO and the large-scale divergent circulation, computed and rendered here daily. Automated objective products; see official outlooks for forecasts.

Madden–Julian Oscillation (RMM)

Wheeler–Hendon (2004) real-time multivariate MJO index. The track shows the last 40 days through phase space; eastward propagation is counterclockwise, and points inside the unit circle are a weak MJO.

Velocity-Potential Anomalies (χ′, T21)

Planetary-scale rising and sinking branches, time-averaged the way velocity potential is meant to be read: daily GFS analyses → χ solved spectrally (T21) → running means (or the 20–100-day MJO-band filter) → anomalies against a 1991–2020 ERA5 climatology computed with the identical solve; the +7/+10-day map sets and the Hovmöller tails use the NCEP GEFS ensemble-mean forecast, always labeled with init and valid time. At 200 hPa, green (negative χ′) marks anomalous upper-level divergence, enhanced deep convection; brown marks the suppressed, sinking branch. The 850-hPa panel shows the low-level return flow: enhanced convection sits over its brown (convergent) centers.

Time-Longitude (Hovmöller) Diagrams

How the tropics are propagating: anomalies band-averaged in latitude and drawn against time (down the page) and longitude. The OLR view overlays Wheeler-Kiladis space-time-filtered contours for the operational wave bands (MJO, Kelvin, equatorial Rossby, MRG/TD-type, low-frequency) - negative (enhanced-convection) contours solid, positive dashed. Circular markers are the first tcvitals fix of each designated tropical cyclone. The newest ~2 weeks of the filtered contours are amplitude-damped by the real-time filter, as each panel states.

Method & data. All graphics on this page are computed and rendered by Triple-A-Tropics. MJO: RMM index values from the Australian Bureau of Meteorology (Wheeler & Hendon 2004); the RMM forecast projects NOAA NCEP GEFS ensemble members (OLR + u850 + u200, out to the model's ~16-day limit) onto the published WH04 EOF structures with the operational preprocessing (seasonal cycle and prior-120-day mean removed on the observation+forecast concatenation), members thin and the ensemble mean bold; the ensemble mean is smoothed by construction and every forecast graphic says so. Velocity potential: daily means of NOAA NCEP GFS analysis winds at 200/850 hPa; χ from a spherical-harmonic Poisson solve of the divergence, triangular truncation T21; running means over the selected window (the MJO view is a 20–100-day Lanczos bandpass whose real-time endpoint is amplitude-damped, as stated on the map); anomalies against a 1991–2020 monthly χ climatology built from ERA5 monthly-mean winds (ECMWF, via the University of Hawaiʻi APDRC) with the identical solve. Hovmöllers: OLR daily means from the NOAA CDR (via NOAA PSL), anomalies vs the same dataset's 1991–2020 daily climatology (mean + first three harmonics); wind from daily GFS analyses vs an ERA5 1991–2020 monthly climatology; χ200 from the velocity-potential product's own daily archive. Every Hovmöller variable (and, as an optional map overlay, χ′ itself) is space-time filtered per Wheeler & Kiladis (1999) with the Wheeler & Weickmann (2001) real-time method; the newest ~2 weeks of any filtered contour are amplitude-damped, as stated on each panel. TC-genesis markers from tcvitals via UCAR/RAL. Automated objective diagnostics: experimental, never official guidance. Updated daily.
