Subseasonal Drivers

The planetary-scale background the tropics ride on: the MJO and the large-scale divergent circulation, computed and rendered here daily. Automated objective products; see official outlooks for forecasts.

Madden–Julian Oscillation (RMM)

Wheeler–Hendon (2004) real-time multivariate MJO index. The track shows the last 40 days through phase space; eastward propagation is counterclockwise, and points inside the unit circle are a weak MJO.

Velocity-Potential Anomalies (χ′, T21)

Planetary-scale rising and sinking branches from the latest GFS analysis: χ solved spectrally from the analyzed wind, truncated at T21, and anomalized against a 1991–2020 reanalysis climatology. At 200 hPa, green (negative χ′) marks upper-level divergence, enhanced deep convection; brown marks the suppressed, sinking branch. The 850-hPa panel shows the low-level return flow: enhanced convection sits over its brown (convergent) centers.

Method & data. All graphics on this page are computed and rendered by Triple-A-Tropics. MJO: RMM index values from the Australian Bureau of Meteorology (Wheeler & Hendon 2004). Velocity potential: NOAA NCEP GFS analysis winds at 200/850 hPa; χ from a spherical-harmonic Poisson solve of the analyzed divergence, triangular truncation T21; anomalies against a 1991–2020 monthly climatology built from NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis 1 (NOAA PSL), interpolated to the analysis date. Automated objective diagnostics: experimental, never official guidance. Updated daily.
