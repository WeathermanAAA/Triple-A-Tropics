    What am I looking at?

    The four-panel chart shows, for the selected season:

    1. Cumulative ACE curve overlaid on the 1991–2020 climatology — nested blue bands for the min–max envelope, the 10th–90th percentile range, and the 25th–75th interquartile range, with dashed cyan and violet lines for the climo median and mean.
    2. Rank trajectory through the season: where this year sat versus every other in West Pacific history at each calendar day (rank 1 = best at the top).
    3. Daily ACE intensity bars, with the season's most active day highlighted in amber.
    4. Storm Gantt timeline: every named system, color-coded by its peak Saffir–Simpson category, sorted from earliest formation at the top to latest at the bottom.

    Pick any past season from the rankings list on the right (sorted by full-season ACE, all the way back to 1945).

    Where does the data come from?

    Historical and provisional track data come from NOAA NCEI's IBTrACS v04r01 dataset. During an active season, the current-season line is kept fresh from live JTWC best-track files as soon as they're issued.
