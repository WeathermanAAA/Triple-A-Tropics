SAR Surface Winds

Storm-tasked synthetic aperture radar ocean-surface winds at ~500 m resolution, the highest-resolution surface wind observation of a tropical cyclone there is. C-band SAR resolves the eye, eyewall and wind maxima structure that scatterometers smooth out. Acquisitions exist only when a storm is tasked, so coverage is per storm and intermittent. Browse past storms below; new passes appear automatically while a storm is tasked. Winds are shown on the standard NOAA/NESDIS/STAR/SOCD SAR wind color scale in m/s.
