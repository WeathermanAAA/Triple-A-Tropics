Observations

Direct measurements of tropical cyclones, straight from the data.

🛩️

Aircraft Recon

Hurricane Hunter missions: the Plan of the Day, flight tracks as wind barbs colored by TC wind, VDM center fixes, dropsondes, and a multi-panel time series. Live during missions.

● Live 💨

Scatterometer

ASCAT-B / ASCAT-C ocean-surface winds as barbs on a TC scale, by basin or per storm, with a satellite backdrop. Resolves the broad gale-force wind field.

● Live 🛰️

SAR Surface Winds

Storm-tasked C-band SAR at ~500 m: the highest-resolution TC surface wind observation there is, indexed per storm. New passes appear only when a storm is tasked.

● Storm-tasked
