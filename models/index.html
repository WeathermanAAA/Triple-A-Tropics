About HAFS

The Hurricane Analysis and Forecast System is NOAA's operational hurricane model, run by the Environmental Modeling Center. Two configurations run each cycle: HAFS-A and HAFS-B, differing in physics and data assimilation. Each uses a storm-following high-resolution nest (~2 km) embedded in a larger parent domain (~6 km), and forecasts to 126 hours. These plots show the fields most useful for intensity and structure at a glance: the pressure field with either the 10 m wind or the composite (column-maximum) radar reflectivity. Plots are rendered straight from the GRIB2 output in the NOAA HAFS AWS Open Data archive every cycle.
