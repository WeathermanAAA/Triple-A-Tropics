Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Andrew Austin ("Triple-A-Tropics," "we," "us" or "our") handles personal information in connection with Cyclone Sandbox, including the web game at triple-a-tropics.com and the Cyclone Sandbox apps for iPhone and Mac (the "Service"). We are the data controller for the account and game data described below.

2. Information Collected and Who Collects It

Guest use. The Service may be used without an account. Guest saves and other game data are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. However, the technical information and analytics described in this Policy are collected from all users of the Service, including guests.

Account information (collected by Clerk, Inc.). If you create an account, our sign-in provider Clerk collects and stores, on our behalf, your email address, username and password (in protected, hashed form), or the name, email address and profile image Google shares if you sign in with Google, together with your IP address, device information and approximate location for security purposes. In the web game, Clerk's sign-in software loads with the page and therefore also receives technical information from guests. See https://clerk.com/legal/privacy.

Account and game data (collected by us). For signed-in users, we store your account identifier, username, account creation date, the saved games you keep in your account and archives of your completed seasons. Your email address and password are held by Clerk, not on our own servers.

Technical information (collected by Cloudflare, Inc.). Cloudflare hosts the Service and automatically collects technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested and the time of the request, to deliver and secure the Service. Cloudflare may set cookies strictly necessary for security. See https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

Apple collects information about App Store downloads under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple ID or payment information.

3. Analytics

We receive aggregated website and server statistics from Cloudflare (such as visits, pages viewed, countries and browser types) and, if you have opted in to share analytics with developers in your device settings, aggregated installation, usage and crash statistics from Apple. On iPhone, Clerk's sign-in software records diagnostic information, such as your device model and operating system version, to operate and secure sign-in. We use no other analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software, and none of this information is used to track you across other apps or websites.

4. Use, Storage and Disclosure

We use personal information only to provide and secure the Service and to understand its aggregate use. Account information, including your email address, is stored by Clerk in the United States. Account and game data are stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure in a database and a private storage bucket, accessible only through authenticated requests for your own account. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We share personal information only with the service providers named above, and where required by law. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising, and your data is not visible to other users.

5. Retention and Deletion

We keep account and game data while your account is active or until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time with the "Delete account" option in the Service, or by emailing us from your account's email address. This permanently removes your account, saved games and season archives; copies in our database's backup history expire after thirty (30) days.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, export of or deletion of your personal information by contacting us, and we will respond within one (1) month. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your information to perform our agreement with you and for our legitimate interest in securing the Service. You may also complain to your data protection authority. Information transferred outside these regions, including to the United States, is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under thirteen (13). You must be at least thirteen (13) to create an account, or sixteen (16) in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. We will delete any account we learn belongs to a person below the applicable age.

8. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy and will post any changes on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Questions or requests: support@triple-a-tropics.com.
