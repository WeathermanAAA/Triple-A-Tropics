What is this?

Triple-A-Tropics is a real-time dashboard for tracking tropical cyclone activity. It ingests official best-track data from NOAA's IBTrACS archive and JTWC's live b-decks, computes statistics like Accumulated Cyclone Energy (ACE), and publishes interactive visualizations.
