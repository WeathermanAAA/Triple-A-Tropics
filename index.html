    What is this?

    Triple-A-Tropics is a real-time dashboard for tracking tropical cyclone activity. It ingests official best-track data from NOAA's IBTrACS archive and JTWC's live b-decks, computes statistics like Accumulated Cyclone Energy (ACE), and publishes the result as interactive visualizations. Subsurface heat products are rebuilt daily from NOAA AOML, with weekly cross-sections and TCHP anomalies layered in from CMEMS ARMOR3D reanalysis.
